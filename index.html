The problem we solve

Most local and informal businesses are invisible online.

They can't afford agencies, don't have the time or the skills, and the tourists and neighbors looking for them never find them. We fix that at the root — as infrastructure, not one-off websites.

How it scales

One platform. Every country. Launched by config, not rebuild.

A new market is a configuration — language, geography, categories, content — not a new codebase. Claude does the heavy lifting: seeding businesses, translations and local guides at scale, with model routing and caching that keep the cost per business near zero.
