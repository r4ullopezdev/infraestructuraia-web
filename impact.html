The gap
Around 2 billion people — ~60% of the world's workforce — earn their living in the informal economy.

Source: International Labour Organization (ILO), Women and Men in the Informal Economy. Most of these businesses have no real online presence: agencies cost more than they earn in a month, and the tourists and neighbors looking for them never find them.

What AI changes

The unit economics of inclusion.

The barrier was never ambition — it was cost. A professional, bilingual presence plus the tools to run it used to require money and skills informal businesses don't have. AI collapses that to near zero.

Without AI

$ hundreds + weeks

Agency fees for a website, copywriting, translation and setup — repeated for every single business. Unaffordable at the scale of a whole country, so it simply never happens.

With Claude

Minutes · ~$0 to the owner

A professional profile, two-language content, guides, quotes and replies generated on demand. Model routing and caching keep the marginal cost per business tiny — so inclusion scales country by country.

Who it reaches

Real people, in real local economies.

Family-run lodgings

Commission-free direct bookings synced to Airbnb — they keep the 15–18% the platforms take.

Market stalls & neighborhood shops

Found in Google in two languages, with a profile they never could have built themselves.

Micro-entrepreneurs

Quotes, bookings and customer replies written for them from a plain-language chat — no tech skills.

Tourism-dependent regions

Bilingual bridges small businesses to high-value English-speaking tourists and expats — money that stays local.

Why Claude

Every API call is a person reached.

Claude is our core model across the whole stack — generating listings, translations and guides for entire countries, and powering the owner-facing tools that let someone with no budget and no technical skills run a professional presence. Because the model does the expensive part, the cost of including one more business is a rounding error. That is the difference between a nice idea and real economic inclusion at scale — and it's why support in API credits translates directly into more businesses brought online, for free.
